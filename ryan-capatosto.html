Ryan Capatosto

Founder & CEO · SaintSal™ Labs

Ryan Capatosto is the founder and chief executive officer of SaintSal™ Labs and the founder of Saint Vision Group LLC. His work brings together artificial intelligence, business operations, and a commitment to helping people move forward with purpose.

Building useful intelligence

At SaintSal™ Labs, Ryan leads the vision for SaintSal™ and HACP™, the Human-AI Connection Protocol. The focus is practical: bringing AI research, creation, building, and automation into the work people already do, with human direction and clear guardrails.

His background spans finance, lending, real estate, and technology. That experience informs a product built for entrepreneurs, teams, and individuals who want support turning an idea into useful work.

Ryan’s work is guided by faith, stewardship, and service. SaintSal™ Labs reflects his commitment to technology that supports people’s goals and the responsibility that comes with building it.
